## Final procedure selection - 2026-11-24

The fully audited repeat regressed from 22/30 to 19/30 reference agreement while stability rose 26/30 to 27/30. Three matches were recovered and six lost; invalid outputs remained 3/150, changing from quote errors to turn errors. Retain kit.v1 only as a supervised aid, restore its exact measured bytes as canonical, and align the six working judge cases to its combined rendering. Keep kit.v1.1 and both raw measurements unchanged; this selection is not a new trial, label change or claim of autonomous gate validity.
